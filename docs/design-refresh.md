# Academy visual refresh

The 2026 refresh retains Google Sans Flex and the academy's original forest green (#244C3B), and applies a shared design system across the public website, learner account and administration workspace.

## Direction
- Spacious typographic hierarchy and generous rhythms; no decorative eyebrow overload.
- Minimal warm-white surfaces on off-white, eucalyptus and sage backgrounds.
- Borderless functional cards with warm diffused elevation, softly rounded corners and clear focus states.
- Original SVG illustrations, a learning-path graphic, custom navigation glyphs and simple text-led interaction.
- GSAP/ScrollTrigger page-entry, heading reveal, staggered cards, visual parallax and line-growth animation with reduced-motion support.
- Accessible mobile navigation and keyboard-friendly skip link, form fields and disclosure areas.
- Dynamic activity filters, payments, authorization and account flows remain operational.

## Complaints content
The public complaint overview, writing guidance and full 11-step procedure reproduce the substance, order and timeframes of **CPD Complaint Management Procedure v1.1**, provided by AU & NZ Migration Academy Pty Ltd. The admin playbook is a staff reference rather than an operational case registry; no complaint details are entered or stored through it.

The published process specifies 3 business days for written acknowledgement, 5 business days from acknowledgement for the initial response, 5 business days for client confirmation, 5 business days for final action review, and possible VCAT referral following 28 days of unsuccessful mediation (or longer by agreement). Mediation costs are shared equally.
